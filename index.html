Help and instructions ↗

Target areas

Load your EuroBillTracker Notes to see your conquered dots. The radar looks for unconquered dots that are eligible in your selected target area. Choose Target area in the expanded radar panel; your choice is remembered on this device.

  • Euro-use area: the Eurozone, other countries and territories using the euro (including Kosovo and Montenegro), and Switzerland. Known unconquerable dots are excluded.
  • All land worldwide: dots marked as containing land by OpenStreetMap data and manual corrections. Access, safety and euro availability are not individually verified.

Places and planning

Inspect places in any dot, or find the next target from your GPS position or a point you choose on the map. Select a listed place or Choose your own point on the map, then open Google Maps. Distances are straight-line.

Places and planning guide ↗

Before you set off

DotPick cannot guarantee euro banknotes in any eligible dot. We do our best to classify dots correctly, but maps can contain mistakes and conditions can change. Check access and safety before setting off, and respect local restrictions.

No dot is worth entering a dangerous or forbidden area. And if your next conquest appears to require a submarine, please question the map before buying one.

Your data

Your Notes CSV is processed on your device and is not uploaded by DotPick. A compact dot profile and settings are saved locally. Online maps use external services.

DotPick does not send your Notes data to Miguel, GitHub or any map provider. Importing your file does not share its contents with them.

Privacy details ↗

Map data

Check times are local. The map revision is independent of the editor version. Background maps may require an internet connection.

Help improve the maps and places

Use Feedback in a dot popup or places panel, or message Miguel / lmviterbo on the EBT forum ↗. Include the dot’s [i, j] reference and what needs checking.

For Euro-use classifications, EBT Target Map ↗ lets you edit a working copy and send a Change Report. Miguel reviews proposed changes before publishing them.
